On the Security of Public Key Protocols
DANNY DOLEV AND ANDREW

Abstract-Recently
the use of public key encryption to provide secure
network communication has received considerable attention. Such public
key systems are usually effective against passive eavesdroppers, who merely
tap the lines and try to decipher the message. It has been pointed out,
however, that an improperly designed protocol could be vulnerable to an
active saboteur, one who may impersonate another user or alter the
message being transmitted. Several models are formulated in which the
security of protocols can be discussed precisely. Algorithms and characterizations that can be used to determine protocol security in these models are
given.
